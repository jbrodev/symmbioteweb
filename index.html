        03 FEATURES
        
A nervous system built to grow on.

        

          Every layer has one job and a stable contract. What ships today is the
          foundation; what compounds over time is the moat — your memory and
          workflows, never flashy autonomous agents.
        

      


      


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Supervisor Orchestration

          
Tasks flow through a deterministic submit → approve → process loop, coordinated by a central orchestrator with state, an event bus, and a task queue.

          core
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
100% Local Inference

          
Powered by Ollama by default. Your prompts, data, and models stay on your machine — no cloud, nothing leaving the device.

          llm
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Human-in-the-Loop

          
Anything outward-facing — email, outreach, automation — waits for your approval. Bounded and deterministic: it asks before it acts.

          safety
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Swappable Providers & Models

          
A stable provider contract behind every backend. Add or switch models from a YAML file, validated against what's installed — with zero framework leakage.

          llm
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Persistent Memory + RAG

          
Embeddings-backed long-term memory and retrieval. The real moat: accumulated workflows and company context that deepen with every task.

          rag
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Local-First Storage

          
Tasks, memory, settings, audit, and traces all live in local SQLite. You own every record — on disk, on your terms.

          storage
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Full Observability

          
Structured logs, an append-only audit trail, and execution trace spans wrap everything consequential — so you always know what it did and why.

          observability
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Plugin-Ready Architecture

          
Modular layers with stable contracts. Add backends, agents, stored entities, or background reactions without rewriting what's underneath.

          plugins
        


        

          

            

              
            

          

          
Native Desktop App

          
Lives in your Windows taskbar with tray controls and hotkeys — always a keystroke away, never a browser tab.

          gui
